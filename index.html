Biography

A career forged in salt, steel, and story.

Joseph Jason Namakaeha Momoa was born in Honolulu, Hawaiʻi, and raised between the islands and Iowa. His heritage — Native Hawaiian, Samoan, and German — has shaped a career defined by authenticity, physicality, and an unmistakable presence on screen.

He broke through as Ronon Dex in Stargate: Atlantis, then redefined a generation's idea of a warlord as Khal Drogo in Game of Thrones. As Arthur Curry / Aquaman in the DC Universe, he carried a franchise across two feature films and billions at the box office.

Off screen, Momoa is a surfer, a father, a cultural advocate for Native Hawaiian and Polynesian communities, and the founder of Mananalu — a water brand built around eliminating single-use plastic.
